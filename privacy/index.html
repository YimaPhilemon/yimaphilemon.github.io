Privacy

What this site collects, and where it goes.

What I collect

If you use the contact form or the WhatsApp or email buttons, I receive what you type: your name, email, and message. Nothing is sent while you are still typing.

If you contribute through the Support page, the name, email and amount you enter are passed to Paystack to take payment. I do not see your card or bank details.

Where it goes

Contact form entries are delivered to my inbox through Web3Forms. The WhatsApp button records the enquiry the same way, so it is not lost if you never press send in WhatsApp.

Payments are handled by Paystack under its own privacy policy.

Nothing you send is sold or shared with anyone else.

Analytics and cookies

This site uses Google Analytics to count visits: pages viewed, rough location (country or city), and device type. Google sets its own cookies for this, under its privacy policy. To opt out, use Google's opt-out add-on or block cookies for this site in your browser.

The site itself stores only your theme choice, a cached exchange rate and your country code for the Support page, all in your browser. None of it is sent to me.

Other services your browser contacts

Loading pages makes your browser request resources from third parties, which can see your IP address: Google Fonts, YouTube for embedded videos, GitHub for the repository stats on the home page, ipwho.is to guess your country, and open.er-api.com for exchange rates.

Questions

To have anything you sent deleted, or to ask about this page, message me on WhatsApp or email yimaphilemon56@gmail.com.
