What This Is, And What It Isn't

Everything on this site is free to look through: the gallery, the project write-ups, the recommended tools list. There is no account to make and nothing behind a paywall. Keeping it online and adding to it costs a little every month, and this page is where anyone who wants to help with that can.

It is not a payment for work. If you are hiring me to build something, that runs through the contact page and gets quoted properly, and it has nothing to do with this page. Chipping in here does not move you up a queue, buy a discount, or come with anything attached.

Contributions are not tax-deductible and are not refundable. Every payment is processed by Paystack, which handles the card details and issues the receipt.

Other Ways To Help

  • Follow or share something on LinkedIn, X or Behance.
  • Tell me what is missing or broken on the site. Corrections are genuinely useful, and the contact form reaches me quickly.
